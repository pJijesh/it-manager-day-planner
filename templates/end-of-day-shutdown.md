# End-of-day shutdown checklist

About ten minutes. The aim is to stop carrying the day in your head.

- [ ] Every task I touched is updated: done, in progress, waiting (with who and since when), or cancelled.
- [ ] Anything promised today is written down with a date, including small promises made in corridors and chats.
- [ ] Open incidents have an owner and a next update time, or a handover note (see `incident-handover-note.md`).
- [ ] Tomorrow's first task and top three are chosen.
- [ ] Anything that slipped today is moved on purpose: new date, delegated, or dropped. None is left to drift.
- [ ] Calendar for tomorrow checked for clashes and for meetings that need preparation.
- [ ] Inbox and chat triaged: nothing important unanswered that I said I would answer.
- [ ] Alerts and backups: anything started overnight (patching, a restore, a migration) has someone watching it.
- [ ] Close the laptop. Note one thing that went well.
