# Decision log entry

Copy one entry per decision into a shared log (a document or a spreadsheet). Keep it short enough that people will actually write it.

- **Reference:** DEC-____
- **Date decided:** ____ / ____ / ______
- **Decision:** (one sentence, in the past tense: "We decided to ...")
- **Decided by:** (name and role)
- **Context:** (what prompted it, in two or three lines)
- **Options considered:** (at least two, including doing nothing)
- **Why this one:** (the main reasons and the trade-offs accepted)
- **Cost and effort:** (one-off and running, if known)
- **Risks and what would make us revisit:** (a date or a trigger)
- **Who was consulted or informed:**
- **Actions:** (owner and date for each)
- **Review date:** ____ / ____ / ______
